# Post 1: Start the clock early

## The Reality Check

Look, if it’s going to take you three months to figure out your business and build it, but it also takes three months to get people to actually know, like, and trust you online... why would you wait to start the second clock?

The biggest mistake you can make right now is staying in "stealth mode" like you're some secret agent.

Building a group of people who care about what you're doing takes time. You can’t rush it, and you can’t buy it at the last minute with ads. The sooner you start showing up, the more people you’ll gather along the way. You want those people around *while* you’re figuring things out, not just on the day you ask them to buy something.

## Pick your sandbox

Don't overwhelm yourself trying to be everywhere at once. Just choose **one** place to start, based entirely on what you're already naturally good at doing:

- **Good with words?** Go with **X (Twitter)** or **LinkedIn**. If you like writing short, sharp thoughts, or sharing quick, interesting industry takes, text is your superpower.

- **Into photos and aesthetics?** Stick to **Instagram**. If your business idea is highly visual—like physical products, design, or food—and you're already snap-happy, this is your spot.

- **Comfortable on video?** Hit **TikTok** or **Instagram Reels**. If you'd rather just talk directly to a camera for 30 seconds instead of typing out a long post, use your voice.

## Don't talk to strangers yet

Once you know which app fits your style, you still don't need to jump out into a room full of strangers and pitch yourself as some grand visionary. That feels awkward, and honestly, nobody likes a fake guru anyway.

Instead, just open an account or update your profile exactly where your friends, classmates, and old coworkers already are.

- **You already have a head start:** You aren't starting from absolute zero. These people already know you.

- **Your built-in support crew:** The people who already like you and want to see you do well are the easiest ones to bring along for the ride. They are the ones who will keep you honest and give you a high-five when you finally get moving.

## What to do next

Don't worry about logos, a fancy business name, or corporate fluff. Just pick that one app and do this today:

1. **Claim your handle:** Lock it down so it’s yours.

2. **Write a normal human bio:** Keep it completely real. Something like: *"Working in [your industry], but using my spare time to figure out how to solve a few annoying real-world problems. Just posting what I learn as I go."*

3. **Turn on the lights:** Drop a quick, one-sentence post letting your people know you're stepping into a building phase.

Stepping away from just scrolling through other people's stuff and actually posting your own journey changes everything. You aren't trying to go viral or become an influencer overnight. You’re just letting your circle know you’re open for business. Get your friends in your corner early—you're going to need them when the real work starts.
