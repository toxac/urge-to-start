## Post 4: Stop Looking for Ideas, Look for Clumsy Workarounds

- **Playbook Reference:** Mission 2, Quest 2, Task 2 ("Log 2 Clumsy Shortcuts You Saw Someone Use").

- **The Friction:** Analysis paralysis—trying to invent a massive, unique, "visionary" idea from scratch.

### The Structure & Key Points

- **The Reality Check:** Fall in love with problems, not ideas. Great businesses are rarely brand-new inventions; they are usually just someone noticing a highly annoying, real-world bottleneck and fixing it.

- **The Concept (The Shortcut Audit):** Stop trying to think of abstract business concepts while staring at a blank wall. Instead, start observing how people around you bypass problems.

- **The Friend-to-Friend Advice:** Look for the "duct tape" in people's workflows. When someone manually copies data across three different spreadsheets, or uses a weird, complicated combination of free tools to finish a basic task, that is a clumsy workaround.

- **The Action Step:** Write down two messy shortcuts you see a coworker or friend use this week. Every ugly shortcut is a neon sign that someone is already desperate for a better solution.
