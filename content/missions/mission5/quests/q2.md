## The Build Dashboard.

This is where you'll live for the next few weeks. Your Build Dashboard is the command center for turning your plan into reality.

**Dashboard Features:**

- **Overview** — Progress, tasks, budget, POC status
- **Tasks** — Kanban board with auto-generated tasks
- **Timeline** — Visual milestones and progress
- **Resources** — Budget tracking and expense management
- **Testing** — Tester management and feedback collection
- **Launch** — Checklist and launch preparation

**Use this until you have something testers can test.**
