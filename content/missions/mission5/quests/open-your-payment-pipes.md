If a customer has to email you to get your bank details or mail you a check, they are going to change their mind and walk away. To make a sale, you have to make buying from you completely effortless.

You aren't ready to test your solution with real users until you have a working checkout button. We need your offer to be "sellable"—meaning a buyer can click a link, type in their card details, and pay you in less than a minute.

We are going to use a standard connection link to set up your payment account. Your only task for this step is to hook up your account, generate a single test checkout link for your offer, and paste it here in your workspace. 

Once that link works, your cash register is officially open.
