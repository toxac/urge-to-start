Building a business requires a clear view of your operational limits. You must audit your Key Activities and Key Resources to understand exactly what it takes to stay alive and deliver on your promises.

Your resources are the absolute essential assets you must possess right now to run your workspace. For a scrappy project, this isn't high-end factory machinery, corporate real estate, or complex patents. It means looking at your physical laptop, your specific skill sets, your available weekly hours, and your remaining risk capital budget. 

Your activities are the primary, repeating tasks you must execute constantly to keep your value engine running. What is your actual daily job going to look like? Is your core activity writing custom software code, manually editing audio assets for clients, curating digital directory listings, or running unscalable customer communication loops?
