## The numbers don't lie.

This is the moment of truth. If the numbers don't work, the business doesn't work. Be honest with yourself.

If the numbers don't quite work, you can iterate. Change the price, change the MSP, change the channel. Just be specific about what you'll change.

Saying 'no' to a bad business is a win. You've saved yourself time, money, and stress. Take what you've learned to the next opportunity.

**Let's decide.**
