You already know who your target five-word customer profile is, but now you need to map out your delivery lines. In business terms, this means locking in your Customer Segments and Channels.

You must stay focused entirely on your core sub-segment of early adopters—the specific subset of people who are experiencing the highest level of pain right now and are actively looking for a workaround. 

Once you know exactly who they are, you must define your delivery channels. This is the explicit physical or digital pipeline you will use to push your offer directly in front of their eyes. You cannot rely on vague aspirations like "social media marketing." You must choose a direct route. Are you cold messaging targets on a professional network, posting inside a locked digital community group, or physically walking through the front door of local independent shops?
