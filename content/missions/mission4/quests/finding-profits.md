To keep your platform alive and build long-term momentum, you must understand your unit economics. This simply means tracking your pricing tiers, your running software costs, and your take-home margins to prove that your engine is mathematically sound.

We break this down across three simple task steps:
1. **Value-Anchored Pricing:** Setting your price tag contextually against the size of the workaround your customer deals with today. Your price should never be based on fear; it must be a direct reflection of the hours or cash you save them.
2. **Tool Expenses Log:** Inventorying your absolute minimum running software costs. This means tracking your domain registry, page hosting, simple database storage, or payment handling setup.
3. **The Break-Even Goal:** Subtracting your running expenses from your core price tiers to find your true take-home profit. This gives you a clear line in the sand: the exact number of monthly sales you must execute just to cover your tool bills.
