While your head is down working on your product or mapping out your service steps, you need to start claiming your public real estate. Do not wait until you are fully finished to make your social media profiles or launch a simple page. If you launch to zero followers, your launch day will be completely silent.

You don't need a massive marketing budget, a fancy agency, or paid advertisements to start building momentum. You just need to document your process openly where your target buyers already hang out. 

Go to the digital watering holes we identified back in Mission 3 and claim your handles, your page names, or your profile slots. Share a quick post introducing yourself and showing a rough behind-the-scenes look at the problem you are actively working to solve. When you show people the messy, honest process of building a solution from scratch, they don't look at it like corporate advertising—they treat it like a human story and want to root for your success.
