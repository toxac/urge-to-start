Every single feature, button, or extra option you try to add to your first version adds another week of delay. It drains your energy and keeps you from facing the market. 

We need to make a strict cut list. Look at your solution and ask yourself: *If I could only give my customer one single button or step to make their pain go away, what would it be?* If you are building a food delivery app, it isn't an AI-powered meal recommendation algorithm; it is just a button that lets them order a specific meal. If you are starting an organizing service, it isn't a custom branding kit; it is you showing up with labels and storage boxes.

Everything else—the settings pages, the user profiles, the premium colors—gets crossed off and saved for next year.
