The biggest enemy of a fast launch is perfectionism. Perfectionism is usually just fear wrapped up in a nice word. You keep tweaking the font, rearranging the layout, or adjusting the package because you are terrified of putting it out there and hearing a "No."

To break this loop, you must define what "acceptable" looks like before you start building. What is the bare minimum state where this product is safe, works, and can be handed to a customer in exchange for money? 

If it securely records the data, it is good enough. If the service cleans the kitchen properly, it is good enough. A rough, slightly ugly product that solves a massive pain point is worth infinitely more than a gorgeous dashboard that does nothing useful.

Write down your exact criteria for a functional version 1.0. Once you hit these baseline lines, you are legally forbidden from adding anything else before launch.
