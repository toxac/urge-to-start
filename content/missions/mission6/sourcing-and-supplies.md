*   **If you are building a service or physical offer:** Your raw materials are your tools, gear, or ingredients. Your bottleneck is lead times, delivery costs, or tracking down an explicit supplier who won't flake on you.

By writing down your stack or your supply list right now, you prevent yourself from getting blindsided by hidden costs or shipping delays mid-build.
