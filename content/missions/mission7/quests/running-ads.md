However, if you have a little bit of risk capital saved up and are wondering how to scale your traffic, this is your safe introduction to running paid ads. Most first-time builders throw $200 at a giant ad campaign, target a massive, generic audience, get zero sales, and assume paid marketing doesn't work. That happens because they are gambling, not testing.

When you're scrappy, you run miniature, low-risk ad experiments using our $5 rule:
*   **Pick a tiny, hyper-focused target audience:** Don't target "all business owners." Target "independent coffee shop managers in Chicago."
*   **Run a $5-a-day test budget:** Set a strict, hard spending limit for a maximum of 3 days.
*   **Test a singular variation hook:** Use the exact short-form video loop or text build-log from Quest 3 that naturally performed well organically.

If those tiny $15 tests yield clicks and trigger an actual sale, you have found a reliable machine where you put $1 in and pull $2 out. If it yields nothing, your budget is completely safe and you cut the test immediately.

Review our automated campaign testing framework below, set up your platform ad accounts, and log your $5 baseline target variables if you want to test the water.
