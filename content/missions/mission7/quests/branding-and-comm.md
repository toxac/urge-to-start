Branding isn't about spending $500 on a corporate logo pack, choosing a luxury color palette, or writing a boring "mission statement." Branding is simply how you present yourself and what people say about you when you leave the room. 

For a scrappy founder, your brand is built entirely on clarity and trust. If your website look is messy, hard to read, or uses fuzzy cell phone images, people will assume your product is sloppy too. If your communication copy sounds stiff, clinical, or acts like a giant corporate board, people will tune you out.

We need to establish your basic communication rules:
1.  **Speak like a helpful peer:** Drop the buzzwords. Don't say "leveraging dynamic synergy." Say "we save your team three hours a day."
2.  **Make it look clean:** Use one clear font, pick two simple contrasting colors, and use high-resolution, un-cluttered screenshots or product images.

When your look matches your plain-spoken message, strangers relax and feel safe buying from your page.

Lock in your simple two-color look, define your standard font, and write out your 3 baseline communication rules below.
