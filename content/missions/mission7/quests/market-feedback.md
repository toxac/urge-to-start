Once your link is out in the wild, the market will start talking back to you. The problem is, the market doesn't speak English—it speaks in numbers and behavior. If you don't know how to decode those signals, you will make emotional decisions and panic.

We need to track two primary indicators to understand exactly how people are reacting to your offer:

*   **The Conversion Metric (The Interest Check):** This is the ratio between the number of unique people who clicked your link and the number of people who actually bought. If your page gets 100 visits and 2 sales, that is a 2% conversion rate, which is completely normal. If you get 200 visits and 0 sales, your product isn't broken—it means your landing text is confusing, your price is scary, or you are sending the wrong type of people to your page.
*   **Qualitative Friction (The Complaining Matrix):** This means tracking the exact words people say when you reach out or when they click around your solution. Write down every single objection: *"It's too expensive," "I don't understand how to upload my file," "Does this work on mobile?"* Those objections are gold. They tell you exactly what you need to fix on your layout page tomorrow to turn the next visitor into a buyer.
