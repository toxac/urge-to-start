#### Track A: Modern Social Platforms (Digital Hooks)
*   **Instagram Reels & TikTok:** Create rapid "Problem-to-Resolution" loops. Show a raw 5-second clip of your customer segment's daily headache happening in real-time, overlay text showing how you solved it, and point them directly to your link.
*   **LinkedIn & Twitter/X:** Write an "Unfiltered Build Log." Share a clean, zero-fluff text block detailing what you discovered about your audience's broken workflow, the exact time or cash they lose to it, and how your tool fixes the math.
*   **Reddit & Forums:** Deploy the "Search-and-Rescue" method. Search active threads for keywords like "how do I fix" or "does anyone know a tool for." Write a detailed, helpful manual solution in the comments for free, and conclude with a link to your tool if they want it done in one click.

#### Track B: In-Real-Life Hustle (IRL Disruption)
*   **The Back-Door Walk-In:** If your solution targets local businesses (like restaurants, coffee shops, salons, or auto shops), walk straight through their front door during their quietest hours. Ask to speak to the shift manager and hand them a single, clean sheet showing how you save them hours or customers.
*   **The Public Square Guerilla Route:** Go exactly where the frustration happens. If you built a tool to dispute local parking tickets, place mock notice cards with a QR code on car windshields. If you built an event directory for college students, hand physical invite cards directly to people standing in line outside local campus spots.
