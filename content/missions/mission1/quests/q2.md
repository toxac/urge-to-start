## You're not starting from scratch.

You have a network. You have skills. You have experiences that are valuable. You just haven't mapped them yet.

Most entrepreneurs think they need to build everything from zero. They're wrong. Your network is your first sales channel. Your skills are your first product.

This quest is about taking inventory:

1. **Your connections** — Who do you know? Who knows people?
2. **Your skills** — What are you actually good at?

By the end, you'll see you're already standing on a foundation. Let's build on it.
