## This is the most important quest.

Not because it's hard. But because it changes everything.

Most people are paralyzed by the fear of "no." They assume rejection means they're not good enough. They stop before they even start.

But "no" isn't a verdict. It's data.

- "No" means you asked.
- "No" means you're trying.
- "No" means you're one step closer to "yes."

Your job in this quest is to go hunting for "no." Not because you want to fail, but because you want to learn. Each "no" is a lesson. Each "no" makes you stronger.

**Let's go get some "nos."**
