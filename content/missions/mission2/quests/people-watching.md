## You are a detective.

Look at the people around you. What problems do they have? What frustrates them? What do they complain about?

Your friends, family, and colleagues are living, breathing problem generators. They're constantly dealing with frustrations—some small, some huge. And most of them don't even realize that what they're experiencing is a business opportunity.

Your job in this quest: Observe. Listen. Take notes.

**Let's watch and learn.**
