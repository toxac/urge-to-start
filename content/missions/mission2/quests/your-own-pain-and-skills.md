The cleanest, lowest-risk business models live inside things you already experience and understand deeply. A tourist has a map, but a local resident knows which alley cuts twenty minutes off the commute, which bus is always late, and which roads to avoid. We want you working with local resident knowledge.

We begin by looking directly at your own weekly routine. Walk through a typical day and look closely for three distinct indicators:
*   **The Time Drain:** The repetitive, boring task you dread because it feels like it should have been automated years ago. (The signal: *"I can't believe I have to do this again."*)
*   **The Clumsy Workaround:** The moments you are bouncing between three different tools or doing manual steps to patch together a single job. (The signal: *"There has to be a better way."*)
*   **The Stress Point:** The low-level anxiety or nagging worry that doesn't go away. (The signal: *"I hope I didn't forget..."*)
