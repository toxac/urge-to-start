## Start with yourself.

The best problems to solve are the ones you understand deeply. Your frustrations are a goldmine.

Think about it: What annoys you? What wastes your time? What feels broken? These are signals. They're pointing to problems that need solving.

This quest has four tasks:

1. **Spot your frustrations** — What annoys you?
2. **Turn them into opportunities** — What could you build?
3. **Audit your skills** — What are you good at?
4. **Skills as a business** — What could you sell?

**Let's dig in.**
