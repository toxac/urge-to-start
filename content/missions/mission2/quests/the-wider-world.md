## Think bigger.

Your immediate circle is just the beginning. The world is full of problems waiting to be solved.

In this quest, you're going to look beyond your friends and family. You're going to explore:

1. **Online communities** — What are people complaining about on Reddit, Facebook groups, Quora?
2. **Trends** — What's changing? What's new? What's growing?
3. **Marketplaces** — What are people already paying for? What's missing?

**Let's go exploring.**
